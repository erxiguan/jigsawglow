How the game handles data on your device, analytics, and ads.

Jigsaw Glow is a jigsaw puzzle game for iPhone and iPad, published by Zheng Qian. This page is the privacy policy for the App Store and for Google AdMob. The game has no account.

Data on this device

Puzzle progress, Memory Match progress, coins, card backs, settings, and your privacy choices stay on the device. They remain until you delete the app. Deleting the app removes them. There is no server copy of this progress.

Product analytics

When collection is allowed, the game sends gameplay, purchase, and ad events to Firebase Analytics. These events describe which albums and puzzles you play, when a Memory Match round finishes, hint and coin changes, and whether a purchase succeeds. They are kept for 2 months and then deleted.

In the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, Firebase does not send these events until you have answered the consent form and allowed the related purposes. Refusing those purposes keeps the events from being sent. Elsewhere, collection is on until you turn off Product analytics in Settings, under Privacy Policy.

Crash reports

When collection is allowed, crash reports are sent to Firebase Crashlytics and kept for 90 days. The same consent and opt-out that control product analytics also control crash reports.

Purchases

Apple processes payments. The game records the product you choose and the price of a successful purchase so membership and coin balances stay correct. That record follows the analytics rules above. Purchase entitlements are managed and verified through Apple's App Store. Some related game state may be stored locally on your device. You may be able to restore eligible purchases with Restore in the store.

Ads

Ads come from Google AdMob. The game can show a rewarded ad for hints and an interstitial ad after some finished puzzles or Memory Match rounds. There is no banner. A subscription removes interstitial ads. An ad request may include the IP address, which Google can use as a coarse location.

In the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, the game does not request an ad until you have answered Google’s consent form and allowed storage on the device. Refusing a purpose means that purpose is not used. Agreeing to the form does not by itself turn on personalized ads. The advertising identifier (IDFA) is used only when the user has consented to both IAB TCF Purposes 3 and 4, and has authorized tracking through Apple's App Tracking Transparency (ATT) prompt. If either is refused, ads are requested without the advertising identifier. The game does not store the IP address or the advertising identifier.

You can open Settings, then Privacy Policy, then Ad privacy choices, to review or withdraw this consent. Withdrawal stops new ad requests that depend on the refused purposes. Google keeps ad data under Google’s privacy policy.

How to withdraw consent or delete data

  • Turn off Product analytics in Settings → Privacy Policy. New analytics events and crash reports stop.
  • In the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, use Ad privacy choices on that same screen to change or withdraw the consent form answers.
  • Delete the app to remove progress, settings, and privacy choices stored on the device.
  • Email kokomoon.guan@gmail.com if you have a question about analytics or crash data. The game has no account, so these reports are not stored under your name or email, and an email cannot be used to find and delete one person’s Firebase events or crash reports. Analytics events are removed after 2 months, and crash reports after 90 days, under those retention periods. Apple’s purchase records cannot be deleted by this request.

Children

Jigsaw Glow is not directed at children.
